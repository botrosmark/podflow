03

Mountain Notes

A few things the mountains will hold you to.

Altitude
Town sits at 7,522 ft; trailheads climb toward 9,000–10,000 ft. Hydrate aggressively, take the first day easy, and watch for headaches or nausea.
Storms
Afternoon thunderstorms are near-daily in August — the 7:30am hike starts are on purpose. Aim to be below treeline by early afternoon.
Temps
Days in the mid-70s to low-80s, nights down to the low-40s. Every hiker (and Millie) needs a full layering kit.
Wildlife
This is elk and black-bear country. Keep 75+ ft from elk and moose, never feed wildlife, and store all food — including picnic leftovers.
Connectivity
Cell service is spotty to none inside RMNP. Download maps offline and tell someone your trail plan each morning.
Leave No Trace
Pack out everything from the three picnics — the trails stay beautiful because everyone does.
